# Taimoor's opening: AI-assisted editorial revision

Imagine a bank customer who moves some savings into an investment account and buys exchange-traded funds, or ETFs. An ETF holds a collection of investments, such as company shares, and its units trade on a stock exchange.

The bank knows which ETFs the customer owns. But which industries and countries do those investments represent?

Providers publish figures such as “30 percent technology.” To interpret that percentage, we need the date it describes, what the category means, and its source. Otherwise, we could combine outdated figures or categories that sound similar but mean different things.

Provider factsheets and product pages already report dated sector and geographic allocations. The Financial Industry Business Ontology, or FIBO, provides a shared financial vocabulary. Earlier work using FIBO, called the Global Fund Reporting Ontology, or GFRO, also demonstrates dated fund information and allocation summaries.

These approaches give us useful building blocks. Shahmeer will now explain the specific gap our project addresses and our proposed model.
