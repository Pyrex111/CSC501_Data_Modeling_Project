Earlier models, including the Global Fund Reporting Ontology, already represent fund information at a specific time. However, the material we reviewed does not explicitly describe our particular combination of a dated provider disclosure, its allocation entries, classification context and captured source. Our project focuses on making those connections explicit.

Looking at our model, we start with the ETF and record a dated allocation disclosure. That disclosure contains entries, such as a reported 30 percent weight, linked to categories such as Technology or Canada.

Each category retains its classification context: the scheme and version when stated, or the provider context when the scheme is unknown. The disclosure also connects to the captured webpage or factsheet supporting the reported percentages.

We plan to represent these connections as a knowledge graph, where funds, disclosures and categories become connected records that computers can query and navigate.

The goal is to keep the fund, date, category meaning and evidence together, so each percentage remains understandable and traceable to its source.
