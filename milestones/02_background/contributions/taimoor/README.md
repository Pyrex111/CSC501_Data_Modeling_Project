# Taimoor Milestone 2 contribution

Project: Extending the Financial Industry Business Ontology to Represent Time-Varying Exchange-Traded Fund Allocations.

This directory contains Taimoor Shahzad's supplied contribution material for CSC501 Milestone 2, Background and Related Work. It is supporting material for the team report, not the final submission archive.

## Files

- `CSC501_M2_Taimoor_Contribution_Draft.docx`: contribution draft, model explanation, relationship notes, modelling rules, competency questions and references.
- `CSC501_M2_Taimoor_ERD_Connectors.drawio`: editable conceptual ER diagram with the revised attached connectors.
- `CSC501_M2_Taimoor_ERD_Connectors.pdf`: one-page ER diagram export.
- `Abstract_and_Introduction.docx`: supplied abstract and introduction draft for integration into the team report.
- `taimoor_m2_contribution.tex`: LaTeX fragment for the team ACM report; not a standalone document.
- `taimoor_m2_references.bib`: bibliography entries used by that fragment.
- `FIBO_evidence.md`: selected definition extracts at a pinned FIBO revision.
- `FIBO_source_manifest.json`: revision and checksum record for the examined source modules.

The evidence supports specific interpretation and reuse decisions. It does not establish that the proposed integrated disclosure pattern is absent from all FIBO modules. SQL and RDF/OWL implementations belong to later milestones.

The final team submission must combine all members' contributions into the required ACM report with two text pages and one ER diagram page, followed by the original-draft appendix where required by the milestone manual.
